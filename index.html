What is IRR?

Internal rate of return is the actual annualised return your money earns over the full investment period, calculated from every cash flow in and out.

It is not the fund's stated performance. It is what your money truly grew at, from day one to exit.

What is effective fee?

Effective fee is the real annual cost drag: the difference between your gross return assumption and your actual IRR, expressed as a percentage per year.

Bonuses and rebates reduce it. Early exit and surrender charges increase it.
